# Privacy Policy for Ink Liar

**Effective date:** 2026-04-20
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

"Ink Liar" (the "App") is a free Android game published by Rastislav
Hodul ("we", "us", or "the Developer"). This privacy policy explains
what data the App collects, how that data is used, and your rights
regarding it.

The short version: **the App collects nothing and sends nothing.** It
runs entirely on your device, has no network access, integrates no
third-party SDKs, and has no advertising.

This version of the policy describes **Ink Liar 1.3.0 and later**.
Earlier ad-supported versions (1.2.0 and below) integrated Google AdMob
and the Google User Messaging Platform; their data flows are described
in the [public Git history](https://github.com/rhodul/ink-liar-legal/commits/master/privacy-policy.md)
of this document.

---

## 1. What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data,
to ourselves or to anyone else, because the App has no ability to
transmit anything:

- The App declares **no `INTERNET` permission**. The Android platform
  therefore blocks every network operation it could attempt.
- The App contains **no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, or
  advertising SDKs**.
- We operate no server, and the App has no user accounts, sign-in,
  cloud sync, leaderboards, or multiplayer features.

## 2. What is stored locally on your device

The App stores the following in Android's DataStore preferences system,
on the device only:

- Your local **high score** (integer).
- Your **game settings**: "Stroop mode" (on/off), "Reduce motion"
  (on/off), "Mute sounds" (on/off), and "Difficulty mode"
  (Easy / Normal / Hard).

This data is never sent anywhere. Its only path off the device is the
Android system's own auto-backup feature, which you control (see
Section 3).

## 3. Android Auto-Backup

If you have Google-account-based automatic backup enabled on your
device (Settings → Google → Backup), the App's locally stored data may
be included in those backups and sent to Google Drive under your Google
Account's private storage. This is standard Android behavior, controlled
by you in your device's system settings. The App does not exempt any
data from backup — nothing it stores qualifies as sensitive information.
You can disable backup for the App in your device's system settings.

## 4. Advertising

The App displays **no advertising**. It does not integrate Google
AdMob, Google UMP, or any other ad or consent-management SDK, and it
carries no advertising ID permission. No advertising identifier is read,
generated, or transmitted.

## 5. What we do NOT do

For the avoidance of doubt, Ink Liar does **not**:

- Require or offer user accounts or sign-in.
- Operate a game server, online leaderboard, or multiplayer feature.
- Run any analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- Access the network at all — the App holds no `INTERNET` permission.
- Integrate with social media, email, contacts, the camera, or the
  microphone.
- Collect health, financial, biometric, or location data.
- Contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
- Sell or share your data with any third party, because no such data
  ever leaves your device under our control.

## 6. Children

Ink Liar is **not directed at children under 13**. It carries an
age-appropriate content rating in the Google Play Store. Because the
App collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from
children.

## 7. Your rights

**Local data.** Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data
from the device. You can also clear the App's data from Android
Settings. (Data in Android Auto-Backup, if enabled, may persist in your
Google Drive until the device is restored without the App, or until you
delete the backup from your Google Account.)

**Everything else.** The Developer holds no data about you, so there is
nothing to access, correct, export, delete, or object to on our side.
Rights of access, correction, erasure, portability, and objection under
the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, and similar laws are therefore satisfied by
the App's design: there is no personal data processing to exercise them
against.

## 8. Data retention

**Local data:** retained on your device until you uninstall the App,
clear its data via Android Settings, or delete associated backups from
your Google Account.

**Everything else:** nothing is retained, because nothing is collected.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes
in the App or in applicable law. Changes take effect on the date posted
as "Last updated" at the top of this document, and a material change
(for example, reintroducing advertising) will be reflected in the Play
Store listing's Data Safety section. Continued use of the App after an
update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Historical versions of this document are available in the
[public Git history](https://github.com/rhodul/ink-liar-legal/commits/master/privacy-policy.md)
of its source repository.
